Screenshot features

  1. Select
    Pick the screenshot area, the right mouse button or the Esc key cancels
  2. Adjust
    • Move the area
      • drag with the mouse
      • the ↑↓←→ keys move the area precisely
    • Resize
      • the +- keys make the area bigger or smaller (Edit menu: square mode, landscape mode, portrait mode), by design the software keeps the area size fixed
      • the Ctrl + ↑↓←→ keys resize precisely
    • Close the area
      • double-click the left mouse button or press Enter
  3. Capture
    Capture the graphics, the window shows the captured content right away
    • a capture happens right after the selection
    • you can keep the adjust window open and capture again and again
    • you can close the adjust window and capture again and again
  4. Export
    Export the graphics
    • the Tools menu opens the matching folder
