Common image ratios and their uses

An image ratio (Aspect Ratio) describes the relation between the width and the height of a picture, for example 16:9 means the width is 16/9 times the height.

The ratio decides the shape of a picture and how it displays on phones, computers, web pages and in videos. Picking the right ratio avoids cropping, stretching or empty space.

At a glance

Scenario First choice
Avatars, product pictures 1:1
Photos 3:2, 4:3
People 2:3, 4:5
Phone wallpaper, short video 9:16
Web pages, video 16:9, 21:9
Top banners 3:1, 4:1

一、Common ratios at a glance

Ratio Shape Common uses
1:1 Square Avatars, social media, product pictures
3:2 Landscape Digital camera photos, landscape photography
4:3 Landscape Traditional photos, tablet screens, slides
5:4 Near square Photography, printed photos
16:9 Widescreen Video, computer wallpaper, web banners
21:9 Ultra-wide Cinema, ultra-wide wallpaper, website banners

9:16 Portrait Phone wallpaper, short video, full screen phone
9:19.5 Tall portrait Modern all-screen phones
4:5 Vertical Social media vertical pictures, half body portraits
2:3 Vertical Portrait photography, posters, phone photography
3:4 Vertical Traditional vertical photos, phone pictures

二、Pick a ratio by scenario

Photos and photography

Ratio Good for
3:2 Digital camera photos, scenery and daily photography
4:3 Traditional photos, daily records and printing
2:3 Full body portraits, vertical photography
4:5 Half body portraits, social media pictures

Phones and mobile devices

Ratio Good for
9:16 Classic portrait screen, phone wallpaper and vertical video
9:19.5 The tall all-screen of modern phones

Note: modern phone screen ratios are not unified, there are also 9:20 and others; for wallpapers it is best to pick the size of the specific phone model.

Web pages and banners

Ratio Good for
16:9 Website background, home page display, horizontal promo images
21:9 Ultra-wide banners, widescreen backgrounds, cinematic display
1:1 Product display, product covers, avatars
3:1 Website top banners, advertising images

Video and social media

Ratio Good for
16:9 The mainstream ratio of YouTube, regular video and web films
21:9 Cinema frames, ultra-wide video
9:16 TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and other short videos
1:1 Square social media video, feed content

三、Ratio and size, what is the difference

  • Image ratio: decides the shape, for example 16:9
  • Image size: decides the pixel dimensions, for example 1920 × 1080
Image size Image ratio
1920 × 1080 16:9
1280 × 720 16:9
1080 × 1080 1:1
1080 × 1920 9:16
1200 × 800 3:2

One ratio can have many sizes; the bigger the size, the more detail it usually keeps, and the bigger the file.

四、Things to watch out for

  1. Do not force stretch: changing width and height without caring about the ratio deforms the picture.
  2. Watch the cropping: when a picture goes into a different container, edge content may be cut off.
  3. Protect the subject: people, text and important objects should not sit where cropping is likely.
  4. Pick the ratio for the device: phones, computers and TVs may have different screen ratios.
  5. Keep the original: when several ratios are needed, crop again from the original high resolution picture.

五、How this relates to RectIcon

The RectIcon selection offers square, landscape and portrait fixed ratio modes. Once a mode is picked, every capture keeps the same width/height ratio, which is perfect for making headers, covers and icons in batches.

六、Summary

  • Photos: 3:2, 4:3
  • Phones: 9:16
  • Web pages: 16:9, 21:9
  • Banners: 3:1, 4:1
  • Video: 16:9 or 9:16
  • Products and avatars: 1:1

There is no best image ratio, only the ratio that fits the scenario best.
