Social media image size quick reference

Different websites have different size rules for avatars, banners and cover pictures. Using the right size avoids blurry, deformed or auto cropped pictures.

All sizes are in pixels (px).

At a glance

Picture type Common recommended size
Avatar 400 × 400 or 800 × 800 (1:1)
Profile banner 1500 × 500 (3:1)
Video cover / thumbnail 1280 × 720 (16:9)
Vertical short video cover 1080 × 1920 (9:16)

III. General size recommendations

If you do not need to match one specific platform, prefer these common sizes:

Picture use Recommended size Ratio
Avatar 800 × 800 1:1
Square picture 1080 × 1080 1:1
Vertical cover 1080 × 1440 3:4
Social media vertical picture 1080 × 1350 4:5
Phone full screen picture 1080 × 1920 9:16
Web link preview 1200 × 630 1.91:1
Landscape video cover 1920 × 1080 16:9
Profile banner 1500 × 500 3:1

IV. Things to watch out for

  1. Avatars: use a square picture, keep the important content centered, consider circular cropping.
  2. Banners: keep important text and logos in the central safe area.
  3. Thumbnails: keep the subject clear, avoid text that is too small.
  4. Cross platform use: every website crops differently, do not use one picture everywhere.
  5. Picture quality: JPG for photos, PNG for logos and transparent pictures.

V. Note

These are common reference sizes, not the forced upload specs of the platforms. Platforms may change display and cropping rules, preview the real result before publishing.

When you need to work out sizes yourself, fix the ratio first and calculate from it: Common image ratios and their uses.
